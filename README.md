# FinGuardian

## India’s First AI-Powered Financial DigiLocker & Legacy Protection Platform

> “Protecting financial identities before they become unclaimed assets.”

# 🚨 The Real Problem
<img width="977" height="537" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-06-13 at 13 33 53" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9bcafce5-a233-4348-ae57-9d9fce8b1d97" />

In India, approximately **₹2.2 lakh crore worth of financial assets remain unclaimed** due to:

* forgotten bank accounts
* inaccessible investments
* missing nominees
* lost documents
* lack of financial awareness within families
* sudden death or emergencies

These unclaimed assets include:

* bank deposits
* mutual funds
* insurance claims
* EPF accounts
* digital financial assets
* property-related records

In most cases, families are unaware:

* where the assets exist
* how to retrieve them
* which liabilities are pending
* what legal procedures are required

As a result:

* assets become hidden forever
* debts create sudden financial pressure
* families suffer legal and financial disputes

Currently, India lacks a unified platform that securely manages a person's complete financial legacy and emergency financial continuity.

---

# 💡 Our Solution — FinGuardian

FinGuardian is India’s first AI-powered Financial DigiLocker & Legacy Protection Platform designed to reduce the growing crisis of unclaimed financial assets.

FinGuardian allows users to securely store:

* financial records
* liabilities
* investment information
* legal documents
* passwords
* digital wills
* emergency instructions

using end-to-end encrypted architecture.

In case of emergencies, verified nominees can securely access important information through a controlled and legally validated recovery process.

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 🔐 Secure Financial DigiLocker

Store lifelong financial information securely including:

* bank accounts
* investments
* insurance policies
* crypto wallets
* property records
* subscriptions
* tax documents

All information is protected using end-to-end encryption.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Nominee-Based Emergency Access

Confidential information is extremely sensitive and cannot be handed over to unauthorized individuals.

FinGuardian ensures access is granted only if:

* the nominee was assigned by the user
* verification protocols are completed
* emergency validation is approved
* legal proof such as a death certificate is verified

This protects highly confidential data such as:

* passwords
* digital wills
* financial credentials
* legal records
* emergency instructions

---

## 📜 E-Will Generation

Users can generate digital wills securely to:

* reduce future family disputes
* simplify inheritance clarity
* organize asset distribution
* ensure financial transparency

---

## 💼 Assets & Liability Management

Users can add:

* assets
* loans
* EMIs
* liabilities
* investments
* recurring obligations

This helps nominees understand:

* available financial resources
* hidden liabilities
* pending debts

because unmanaged debts can severely damage a family’s financial stability during emergencies.

---

## 📊 Financial Insights & Risk Detection

FinGuardian analyzes user financial health and identifies:

* financial risks
* insurance gaps
* debt overload
* inactive investments
* recurring financial leakages

allowing users to take corrective actions early.

---

## 📁 Vault Locker

A secure vault locker for storing:

* property documents
* IDs
* insurance papers
* wills
* tax documents
* financial agreements

This helps nominees recover lost or inaccessible documents and retrieve financial assets more easily.

---

## 🤖 AI Guardian

AI Guardian acts as an intelligent assistant that:

* explains financial information
* simplifies recovery procedures
* guides nominees during emergencies
* summarizes liabilities and assets
* helps users understand financial risks

---

## 🚨 Simulated Emergency Access

FinGuardian’s most important innovation.

The platform simulates emergency recovery workflows and securely transfers access to verified nominees during real-life mishappenings.

The system ensures:

* controlled staged access
* nominee verification
* emergency approval workflows
* protection against unauthorized access
